FINAL PRODUCT SPECIFICATION
================================================================================

PRODUCT CONCEPT
A mobile cybersecurity education app for iOS and Android where users learn by safely experiencing realistic traps. The app does not teach through lectures first. It lets the user make a normal decision, stops the flow, reveals the risk, shows what could have happened, explains the safer behavior, and ends with a local-only data receipt.

CORE LOOP
1. Experience
2. Trust something normal
3. Stop the user before harm
4. Reveal the trap
5. Reassure that nothing was sent/stored
6. Explain what could have happened
7. Show session-only data receipt
8. Teach safer alternative
9. Use a second twist or challenge
10. Clear session data

TONE
- Surprising, shocking, memorable.
- Not panic-inducing.
- Never tell users “we hacked you” or “we captured your password.”
- Always quickly state: nothing was sent, nothing was stored, and the session is local-only.

GLOBAL SAFETY REQUIREMENTS
- Never collect real passwords.
- Never send lesson inputs to a server.
- Never store sensitive lesson input after the report.
- Never use analytics SDKs for the session demo data.
- Never create exact phishing pages that can be deployed as credential collectors.
- Keep all demo inputs in memory only.
- Mask typed email in reports.
- Never show or retain the password.
- Make “Sent: 0 / Stored: 0 / Real credentials: 0” visible.
