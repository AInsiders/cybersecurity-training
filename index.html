Lesson 2
A QR code can look safer.

After the fake-login lesson, this second stage claims a QR code is a more secure way to access the website. The twist: a QR code is only safer if it points to a real, verified login flow.

Secure Google Sign-In
Scan to securely access our website with Google

Hold up again.

You trusted the QR code because it was described as safer.

This stayed in the browser. Nothing was sent. Nothing was stored. Your phone was not accessed.

  • A QR code is encoded instructions, often a link.
  • It can open a real OAuth login or a fake login page.
  • It can redirect, track, prompt permissions, or lead to a fake update/app install.
  • The safe part is not the square. The safe part is the verified destination and login method.
